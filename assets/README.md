## 7. 3D CGI Studio Product Renders & Turntables (52 Products)

### Geometric Accuracy & Engine Specifications:
- **Projection:** Standard architectural 3/4 axonometric perspective (azimuth 28°, elevation 0.50 foreshortening).
- **Scale:** Calibrated 4.2 px/mm scale, supersampled at 2400×1920 (2x) and Lanczos-downsampled to 1200×960.
- **Glass Pane:** 12 mm Optiwhite float glass with accurate seating depth (`glassDepth`), polished beveled top edge, diagonal studio softbox reflection, and characteristic emerald-teal refraction along the front cut edge.
- **Base Geometry:** Strictly respects `spec.baseWidth`, `spec.overallHeight`, `spec.glassDepth`, and `shape` (rect, arch, taper, led, round).
- **Bracket Systems (BRT):** Rendered as 2 separate brackets mounted along a continuous glass pane, revealing the floor gap between them.
- **Side Mount Systems (SD):** Rendered with fascia-mounting flanges and countersunk anchor bolts.
- **Handrails:** Rendered capping the top polished edge of the glass pane with authentic circular or rectangular cross-sections.
- **LED Channels:** Integrated glowing rope-light channel on PR 21 CP LED and PR HR-7135 LED with radiant optical spill.

## 8. Profile Accuracy & Integrity Report

Every profile in `js/data.js` has been rendered according to its exact dimensional constraints:
- **No profiles were omitted or guessed**: All 34 railing systems (PR 01 to PR 34) and all 18 handrails (PR HR-42 to PR HR-2017) are fully rendered and verified.
- **No artificial features added**: LED channels are strictly restricted to `PR 21 CP LED` and `PR HR-7135 LED`.
- **Brackets rendered as pairs**: All 9 bracket profiles (`PR 23 BRT` to `PR 31 BRT`) correctly showcase twin discrete brackets anchoring a continuous glass sheet.
- **Dynamic Fallback**: If an image is missing or a user uploads a new custom profile without an image, `productVisual()` seamlessly falls back to the SVG parametric cross-section without breaking layout.
