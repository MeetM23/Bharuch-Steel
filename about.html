FAQ

Good to know

What glass thickness do the systems take?

Most systems are designed for 12 mm toughened glass. Selected profiles (PR 02, 05, 11, 19, 31) accept 13.5 mm up to 17 mm glass – check each product page for details.

How far apart are the bolts?

Continuous profiles are anchored with bolts at every 18 inches (12 inches on slim profiles like PR 12 and PR 13). Bracket systems use one bracket every 2.5 ft.

Which finishes are available?

Anodised satin silver, realistic sublimated wooden grain, and any RAL powder-coat shade in matt or gloss.

Do you publish prices?

Pricing depends on running length, glass, height and finish, so every project is quoted individually. Use Get a Quote on any product and we'll respond quickly.
